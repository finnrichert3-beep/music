# Auburn Music Audition — Repertoire

## Audition requirements
- Two contrasting pieces, performed from memory, representing different musical styles (Baroque, Classical, Romantic, Impressionistic, Contemporary)
- Sight-reading
- Full pieces expected, not excerpts

## Pieces

### 1. Passacaglia — Handel / Halvorsen (Baroque)
- Known by ear from tutorials; working on reading it from notation
- Arrangements available at multiple difficulty levels
- Full piece, ~4–5 min depending on arrangement

### 2. Gymnopédie No. 1 — Erik Satie (Impressionistic)
- Locked in as the second piece, replacing Van Gogh (Virginio Aiello)
- Standalone piece (not an excerpt from a larger suite, despite being grouped with Gymnopédies No. 2–3)
- Easy arrangements widely available; left hand is a repetitive octave/chord pattern, right hand is a simple melodic line — good fit for someone still building notation-reading skill
- Full piece, ~3 min

## Notes
- Also known by ear (not yet audition candidates): Van Gogh (Virginio Aiello), Woke Up in Japan (friqtao) — useful for extra reading practice
- Prep plan is phase-based (bass clef recognition → sight-reading → polishing from notation), not date-locked; started Sept 21, 2026
